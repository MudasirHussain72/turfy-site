Turfy: Paint the World

Privacy Policy · Effective 26 October 2026

Turfy is a casual mobile game made by DevTrail ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: Turfy has no accounts, your game progress stays on your device, and the only data that leaves your phone is what is needed to show ads and process purchases.

Information we handle

Game progress. Coins, skins, world progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not upload them and cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising. Turfy shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown; you can decline and still play, with non-personalised ads. On iOS you may also see Apple's tracking prompt. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your card or bank details. To unlock what you bought and to restore purchases on a new device, purchase receipts and a random, anonymous app identifier are sent to RevenueCat, our purchase-validation provider. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Nothing else. Turfy does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and does not use a separate analytics SDK.

Why we use it

  • To show ads that keep the game free, and to measure ad performance.
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the game working and fix problems.

Your choices

  • Ads consent: change your choice at any time from the game's Settings where a consent option is shown, or through your device's ad settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: the "Remove Ads" purchase stops ads between matches permanently.
  • Reset: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data.
  • Requests: email us (below) to ask about, or request deletion of, any data our providers hold that relates to your device.

Children

Turfy is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Our providers retain ad and purchase data according to their own policies linked above. Data sent to those providers is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be shown inside the game.
