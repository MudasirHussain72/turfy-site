Turfy: Paint the World

Support

Turfy is a quick, colourful turf-capture game: swipe to steer, loop back home to claim ground, and splat anyone who crosses your trail. Play against bots offline, unlock 3D skins, and conquer six candy worlds.

Need help?

Email mudasir7t1@gmail.com and include your device model and what happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads or coins but didn't get them. Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Can I move my progress to a new phone? Progress is saved on the device only, so it doesn't transfer yet. Purchases can always be restored.

How do I stop seeing ads? The "Remove Ads" purchase in the shop stops ads between matches permanently. Rewarded ads (revive, bigger start) are always optional.

How do I change my ads privacy choice? Use the consent option in the game's Settings where available, or your device's ad settings.
